5 Prayers Widget — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

5 Prayers Widget ("the app") shows Islamic prayer times, an athan (adhan) alert, a Qibla direction and related information on your Android home screen, lock screen and notification bar. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Summary

  • The app works fully offline. Prayer times are calculated on your device.
  • Your location is used only on your device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is never uploaded to us.
  • We do not have user accounts and do not collect names, emails or contacts.
  • The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID. Buying Premium removes ads.

Location

If you tap "Use my GPS location", the app asks Android for your approximate/precise location once, stores the resulting coordinates and city name on your device, and uses them to calculate prayer times and the Qibla bearing. You can instead type a city name; that search is sent to a public geocoding service to look up coordinates, and only the city text you typed is transmitted. Location data is not sent to our servers (we do not operate any) and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (free version)

The free version displays banner ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect the device's advertising ID, coarse location derived from IP address, and device information to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's policy. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before any ad is loaded, and you can change your choice at any time from the app's settings ("Privacy options"). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Purchases

Premium is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play Billing. We receive only a purchase token confirming the unlock; payment details stay with Google.

Notifications

With your permission the app posts local notifications (prayer reminders, athan playback control and an optional prayer-times bar). These are generated on the device; no notification service of ours is involved.

Data we store on the device

Your settings (city and coordinates, calculation method, widget colours and options, premium status) are stored in app-private storage on your device and are removed when you uninstall. Android's automatic backup may include them in your Google account backup according to your device settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
